工頭血壓飆高了！Foreman Breakdown!AEML, NTUST

      
工地亂成一團，工頭的血壓已經衝破 200！選好你的角色，放炸彈、炸開方塊、收集道具，成為最後站著的工人！The site is in chaos and the foreman's blood pressure just passed 200! Pick a character, drop bombs, blast blocks, grab power-ups and be the last worker standing!

      
模式Mode
        

      

        
        
        
        
      

      
對戰：2–4 位（玩家或電腦），最後存活者贏得回合；剩 1 分鐘進入「驟死」，鋼板由外向內落下。點 P1–P4 切換 玩家/電腦/關閉；按「選擇角色」換角色；手機連上後該位置自動變成玩家。Battle: 2-4 players (human or CPU). Last one standing wins the round; with 1 minute left, sudden death drops plates from the outside in. Tap P1-P4 to cycle Human/CPU/Off; use Characters to pick who plays; a connected phone turns its slot into a human.

      
關卡（參考 Bomberman II 的 NORMAL MODE）：每個 AREA 有 4 關，每關 200 秒、3 條命。消滅所有敵人後，找到藏在方塊下的出口。炸到出口會引來更多敵人！1–4 位玩家合作。Normal mode (after Bomberman II): 4 stages per AREA, 200 s per stage, 3 lives. Defeat every enemy, then find the exit hidden under a block. Blasting the exit spawns more enemies! 1-4 players co-op.

手機／電腦連線對戰Phone / PC multiplayer

      
手機玩家掃描 QR Code；用電腦的玩家按「複製網址」後傳給他（LINE、Email 皆可），在他的電腦瀏覽器開啟即可。每位玩家都會看到遊戲畫面，可用鍵盤、手把或觸控操作；依加入順序成為 P1–P4，也可自行改選。走雲端中繼時，不在同一地點也能一起玩。Phone players scan the QR code; for players on a computer, press Copy link and send it (LINE, email...) to open in their browser. Everyone sees the game and can use keyboard, gamepad or touch; players take P1-P4 in join order and can pick another slot. Over the cloud relay, players can be anywhere.

      
------

      

      

      

      

      
連線方式與 Wii 模擬器相同：有設定 data-relay 時走 Cloudflare 雲端中繼（任何網路都通）；否則先嘗試 WebRTC 直連，7 秒內連不上自動改走網路中繼。手機斷線 30 秒內回來仍是原本的玩家。Same link as the Wii emulator: with data-relay set it uses your Cloudflare relay (works on any network); otherwise WebRTC direct first, falling back to the network relay after 7 s. A phone that drops and returns within 30 s keeps its player.
