爆破工地Blast Site

      
放炸彈、炸開方塊、收集道具，成為最後站著的工人！Drop bombs, blast blocks, grab power-ups and be the last worker standing!

      
模式Mode
        

      

        
        
        
        
      

      
對戰：2–4 位（玩家或電腦），最後存活者贏得回合；剩 1 分鐘進入「驟死」，鋼板由外向內落下。點 P1–P4 切換 玩家/電腦/關閉；手機連上後該位置自動變成玩家。Battle: 2-4 players (human or CPU). Last one standing wins the round; with 1 minute left, sudden death drops plates from the outside in. Tap P1-P4 to cycle Human/CPU/Off; a connected phone turns its slot into a human.

      
關卡（參考 Bomberman II 的 NORMAL MODE）：每個 AREA 有 4 關，每關 200 秒、3 條命。消滅所有敵人後，找到藏在方塊下的出口。炸到出口會引來更多敵人！1–4 位玩家合作。Normal mode (after Bomberman II): 4 stages per AREA, 200 s per stage, 3 lives. Defeat every enemy, then find the exit hidden under a block. Blasting the exit spawns more enemies! 1-4 players co-op.

手機連線對戰Phone multiplayer

      
每位玩家用手機掃描 QR Code：手機就是搖桿，也會顯示遊戲畫面。依連線順序成為 P1–P4，可在手機上改選。Each player scans the QR code: the phone is a controller and shows the game. Phones take P1-P4 in join order and can pick another slot.

      
------

      

      

      

      

      
連線方式與 Wii 模擬器相同：有設定 data-relay 時走 Cloudflare 雲端中繼（任何網路都通）；否則先嘗試 WebRTC 直連，7 秒內連不上自動改走網路中繼。手機斷線 30 秒內回來仍是原本的玩家。Same link as the Wii emulator: with data-relay set it uses your Cloudflare relay (works on any network); otherwise WebRTC direct first, falling back to the network relay after 7 s. A phone that drops and returns within 30 s keeps its player.
